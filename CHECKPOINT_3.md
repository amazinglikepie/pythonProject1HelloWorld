   0.28 / 0.42 on 2026-01-13 and modelpricewatch carries the same figure, so the tracker row was stale.

5. DeepSeek V4 Flash and Pro: the catalog changed 2 to 3 days after the tracker on 12 days around the August changes.

**Against modelpricewatch's own verified change events (2026-06-25 onward).** 8 events, all name-matched, 6 with a
corresponding catalog change. Lag of the catalog behind the site, in days: -1, -1, +1, +3, +3, +4. The two without a
catalog change: Gemini 2.5 Flash on 2026-07-04, where modelpricewatch moved from 0.075 / 0.30 to 0.30 / 2.50 while the

market move); and GLM-5.3-Flash on 2026-09-09, not yet in the catalog by 2026-09-13

**Against the tracker's curation dates.** For the GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna cuts the catalog date is 2026-07-31,
modelpricewatch's is 2026-08-01, the tracker's is 2026-08-17. So in 2026 the catalog's dates are within days of the
best available reference and the tracker's are the laggard. For 2024 to 2025 no independent dated reference is in hand,
so the catalog's lag in that period is unmeasured; this stays a limitation.

## 6. Judgment calls made in this stage

- Daily sampling of the catalog (last commit per UTC day); intra-day churn ignored.
- Vendor-own means provider label and model family both belong to the vendor.
- Context-length tiers of one model collapsed to the largest tier; duplicate spellings collapsed to one key.
- List price is the primary series; promotional prices (Qwen3.7-Max, GPT-5.6 Sol) will be a sensitivity check.
- Tracker rows identified as yuan-as-dollars (MiniMax) are excluded from price comparisons and kept for the
  domestic-versus-international check.
